## My Contribution

## How Trust Anchor Works

### 1. Search Seller
The buyer enters the seller's name, phone number, or Trust Anchor ID.

**What happens next:** Trust Anchor displays the seller's profile and reputation information.

### 2. Review Seller Reputation
The buyer checks the seller's Trust Score, verified sales, ratings, reports, and verification status.

**What happens next:** The buyer uses this information to decide whether the seller appears trustworthy before making payment.

### 3. Generate Transaction Code
When a buyer and seller agree on a transaction, the seller generates a unique Trust Anchor transaction code.

**What happens next:** The transaction is registered on Trust Anchor and can be confirmed by the buyer.

### 4. Verify Transaction
The buyer enters the transaction code and confirms the purchase.

**What happens next:** The transaction becomes a verified transaction on the seller's profile.

### 5. Leave Feedback
After completing the transaction, the verified buyer can rate and review the seller.

**What happens next:** The feedback contributes to the seller's reputation and helps future buyers make safer decisions.
