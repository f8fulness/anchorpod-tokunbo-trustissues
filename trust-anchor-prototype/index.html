THE PROBLEM

Buying a second-hand phone should not be a gamble.

Buyers often meet sellers they do not know and have little reliable information to check before making payment.

?

Unknown Sellers

A buyer may have no reliable way to know the seller's previous transaction history.

!

Hidden Risk

Complaints, previous reports and bad transaction experiences may not be visible to a new buyer.

₦

Payment Pressure

Buyers may be pressured to make payment before they have enough information to make a confident decision.

The question Trust Anchor answers: “What can I actually check about this seller before I pay?”
HOW IT WORKS

From seller search to safer decisions.

Trust Anchor gives buyers a simple four-step process.

01

Search Seller

Enter the seller's name or Trust ID to find their Trust Anchor profile.

→
02

Check Reputation

Review the seller's trust score, verified sales, ratings and reports.

→
03

Verify Transaction

A unique transaction code can be used to register and confirm a transaction between buyer and seller.

→
04

Leave Feedback

After a verified transaction, the buyer can leave feedback that contributes to the seller's reputation.

WHY TRUST ANCHOR?

Trust benefits both sides of the transaction.

FOR BUYERS

Something real to check.

Buyers can look at a seller's reputation before deciding whether to continue with a transaction.

  • Seller identity and Trust ID
  • Transaction history
  • Ratings and feedback
  • Reports and reputation
FOR SELLERS

A reputation you can build.

Honest sellers can build a visible transaction history that makes it easier for future buyers to trust them.

  • Build buyer confidence
  • Show successful transactions
  • Collect verified feedback
  • Grow seller reputation
